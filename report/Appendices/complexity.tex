\section{Computational Complexity}\label{app:complexity}

We measure computational cost in terms of cryptographic operations. 
The client performs \bigO{m \, t} elliptic-curve operations to split $m$ addresses and secrets into $t$ shares. 
Each STTP processes the header in layers, leading to quadratic complexity \bigO{m^{2}} per STTP, though parallel execution of STTPs mitigates this overall latency.
Network nodes perform only linear-time operations.
